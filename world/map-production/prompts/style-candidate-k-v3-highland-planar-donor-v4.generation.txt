Create one orthographic top-down antique fantasy-atlas dry-highland print texture donor. Fill the canvas edge to edge with warm pale parchment and restrained ochre. Across the plate, place a small number of organically distributed dry zones made only from sparse SOLID pinprick ink dots, tiny SOLID angular chips, and very short isolated non-directional dry-brush flecks. Leave more than half of the canvas nearly empty. Broad highland structure must be visible only through irregular changes in the density of these solid micro-marks.

Every mark must be filled and compact. Absolutely no hollow marks, outlined pebbles, bubbles, circles, rings, loops, cells, honeycomb, rosettes, tree crowns, round stains, contour forms, mountain symbols, peaks, ridges, slope strokes, hatch bundles, rows, grids, repeated dashes, terraces, shadows, lighting, perspective, large solid polygons, soft clouds, camouflage, blurred noise, watercolor blooms, labels, roads, rivers, coastlines, borders, buildings, frames, or vignettes. Do not make photographic soil or stone.

Use matte low-to-medium-contrast printmaking. Marks should be crisp, irregularly sized from pinprick to a few pixels, independently ending, and free of alignment or repetition. Quiet areas must retain subtle natural paper grain. The plate is a texture donor only; no semantic geometry and no text.
