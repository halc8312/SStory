Create one orthographic top-down antique fantasy-atlas dry-highland interior texture plate. Fill the entire canvas edge to edge with restrained warm ochre, pale umber, and aged parchment. Represent elevated dry ground only as sparse irregular archipelagos of crisp tiny mineral stipple, fractured dry-ink grain, and a few small flat ochre flecks, separated by very large calm parchment clearings. Broad variation must come from the density of those fine marks, not from large solid color polygons or soft shading. At overview scale the plate should show several organically distributed dry mineral zones; at close zoom the marks must remain crisp, flat, and non-directional.

No mountain symbols, peaks, ridges, ridge chains, contour lines, nested bands, slope strokes, hatching, repeated dash bundles, rows, terraces, cliffs, shadows, lighting, perspective, circles, loops, rings, round stains, watercolor blooms, soft clouds, camouflage, blurred noise, solid low-poly patches, labels, roads, rivers, coastlines, borders, buildings, frames, or vignettes. Do not make photographic rock or soil.

Use matte low-to-medium contrast dry printmaking. Keep at least half of the canvas visually quiet. Marks must be tiny, filled rather than outlined, irregularly spaced, and free of visible cadence or repeated tile structure. The plate is a texture donor only; it must contain no semantic geometry and no text.
