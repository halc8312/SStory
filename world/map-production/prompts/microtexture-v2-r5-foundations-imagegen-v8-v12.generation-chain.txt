MICROTEXTURE V2 R5 CONTROL FOUNDATIONS — IMAGEGEN V8–V12 CHAIN

Role
----
Fresh, Vision-qualified reference foundations for the r5 blinded synthetic
calibration and holdout controls. These images are not production art, a
Golden input, a texture donor, or final pixels. The r4 foundations, controls,
labels, key, and measurements were not used as r5 authority inputs.

Step 1 — v8, rejected by Root Vision
------------------------------------
Create one production-calibration reference image only: a seamless flat orthographic material field for a premium hand-painted fantasy map ground. Warm ivory–gold parchment color, restrained natural mineral/paper variation, extremely smooth and calm at close zoom. The usable center and all edges must be visually clean and evenly illuminated. Allow only very broad, low-contrast tonal drift with characteristic scale larger than roughly 100 pixels. Absolutely no map geography, coastlines, roads, rivers, borders, labels, symbols, objects, frames, vignette, gradients toward edges, folds, stains, blotches, clouds, rings, halos, brush marks, scratches, fibers, crosshatching, stippling, dots, specks, grain, halftone, repeated texture, short lines, parallel lines, or sharp micro-detail. No embedded text. Natural but nearly featureless, suitable as a clean baseline that remains artifact-free when inspected at 200% and 400%. Landscape 3:2 composition, target 1536×1024, opaque RGB.

Decision: rejected. The full-200 and quadrant-400 views exposed a subtle
diagonal lattice/repeating texture. The raster is not tracked or authoritative.

Step 2 — v9, rejected after independent review
------------------------------------------------
Generate a completely new clean calibration baseline, not a variation of the previous result. Premium fantasy-map ground color only: a flat matte warm ivory-gold digital wash, landscape 3:2, target 1536×1024, opaque RGB. It must remain visually featureless under nearest-neighbor enlargement to 400%. Remove all paper texture and all fine material detail. Permit only one or two extremely broad, irregular, non-repeating tonal drifts whose scale is greater than 300 pixels and whose contrast is barely perceptible. No diagonal pattern, checker pattern, lattice, weave, fibers, grain, noise, stipple, dots, specks, mottling, cloudy blobs, watercolor blooms, rings, bands, brush marks, scratches, short lines, parallel lines, halos, stains, folds, vignette, edge shading, objects, symbols, geography, labels, or text. Uniform illumination and clean center/edges. Think smooth low-noise studio color field created with a very large soft airbrush, not photographed paper.

Decision: rejected. One blind-independent reviewer reproduced the Root concern
that the full-200 view contained faint diagonal/mesh regularity and scored it
92. The copied workspace raster was moved to tmp/rejected-imagegen and the
original generator output remains outside the repository authority graph.
