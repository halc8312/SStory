Phase 5 cartographic material atlas v1 — normalized production brief

PROVENANCE NOTICE

This is a post-generation normalized production brief. It is not a verbatim
raw ImageGen invocation transcript. The exact raw invocation prompt, exact
model identifier, generation identifier, and generation timestamp were not
retained. Those absences are recorded explicitly in the adjacent generation
receipt and must not be inferred from this brief.

INTENDED OUTPUT

Create one unlabeled 1536x1024 landscape RGB PNG that functions as a material
atlas for a strict orthographic fantasy-map renderer. The image should be a
continuous copperplate-like field without panels, captions, dividers, border,
signature, watermark, legend, compass, scale bar, text, or pseudo-text.

MATERIAL VOCABULARY

- Connected overhead forest canopy: irregular merged crowns, branching gaps,
  fine stipple, varied edge density, olive and restrained sepia ink.
- Cultivated land: dense hand-drawn field hatching with locally changing
  direction and spacing; avoid machine-perfect repetition.
- Wetland: shallow plan-view water and marsh texture, broken reed density and
  fine horizontal water marks, without scenic depth.
- Neutral parchment: quiet warm paper grain that tolerates low-strength use.
- Flat rock hachure: short disconnected direction-neutral ink marks suitable
  for plan-view geology, never peaks or terrain faces.

STYLE AND HARD EXCLUSIONS

Use warm muted parchment, restrained olive/sepia ink, high local line detail,
and no lighting model. Exclude perspective, isometric view, façades, side
faces, cast shadows, directional highlights, mountains as triangles or cones,
dimensional rock piles, repeated grass-bundle stamps, repeated circular tree
stamps, modern objects, readable writing, letters, numbers, logos, signatures,
and watermarks.

POST-GENERATION USE POLICY

The generated image is not accepted as a whole-image style reference. Only the
five canonical Phase 5 crops and the separate H9-specific high-frequency crop,
all hash-locked in the adjacent receipt, are approved as source material. The
H9 crop is approved only for its recorded 0.08-strength inter-canopy gap fill;
it is not a general Phase 5 material crop. The receipt's rejected zones must
never be sampled. Any renderer must clip use to a canonical semantic mask, keep
strength within its own locked limit, and preserve protected geography.
