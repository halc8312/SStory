Step 1 — v15, adopted foundation
---------------------------------
Generate a brand-new independent 3:2 QA control-foundation image, target
1536×1024, opaque RGB. Create an extremely smooth warm-ivory,
parchment-like color field; “parchment-like” describes the restrained warm
palette only and must not introduce a physical paper surface. Allow only
barely perceptible, irregular, non-repeating, broad low-frequency tonal
variation. Keep the center and every edge calm and evenly illuminated.
Strictly exclude all microtexture, grain, noise, dither, dots, specks, pores,
stipple, bounded blobs, mottling, cloudlike patches, blooms, fibers,
scratches, brush marks, short lines, ridges, parallel lines or bundles,

contours, repeated texture, seams, panels, borders, frames, vignette, edge
shading, halos, ringing, compression artifacts, text, labels, symbols,
objects, geography, or terrain. Produce a clean low-noise digital color field.
