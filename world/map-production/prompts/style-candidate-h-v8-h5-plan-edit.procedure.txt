Candidate H8 deterministic H5 plan-edit procedure (frozen before rendering)

Purpose
Create a diagnostic Golden-reference candidate by editing only the documented
defects in the SHA-256 locked Candidate H5 raster.  This is a deterministic
localized raster edit, not an ImageGen reroll and not a replacement map redraw.

Locked parent
world/map-production/candidates/style-candidate-h-v5-strict-plan-symbols.png
SHA-256: d95ea917ee2b0a414c3e32de762208af4fb2239d7bbc65fa7633e85218ad56fe

Permitted edits
1. Replace the central city's wall, keep, tower, and roof imagery with dense,
   unshaded, orthographic building footprints, lanes, streets, courtyards, and
   uniform plan rings.  No facade, side face, modeled height, cast shadow, or
   directional highlight may be drawn.
2. Replace the south-west port buildings and piers with flat plan footprints.
3. Replace detected repeated olive canopy stamps with merged, seeded,
   non-cloned irregular overhead canopy unions, clearings, branching marks,
   stipple, and broken copperplate strokes.
4. Add only local copperplate microtexture within those semantic edit masks.

Protected parent material
Ocean, coastline, delta and river channels, field plots, highland, and road
network pixels remain byte-identical except where the explicit port footprint
crosses water.  Candidate H5 itself is never overwritten.

Forbidden processing
- no ImageGen reroll
- no H7 pixels or geometry
- no histogram or rank transfer
- no whole-image remapping, blur, emboss, relief, lighting, or shadow
- no cloned/repeated canopy stamp silhouette
- no text, pseudo-text, frame, legend, compass, watermark, or modern object

Frozen automated gates
- locked-parent SHA-256 and 1536x1024 RGB PNG contract must pass
- every changed pixel must be inside the emitted semantic edit-mask union
- at least 88% of detected old canopy-stamp pixels must change
- at least 86% of the old city core and 62% of the port repair mask must change
- total changed-pixel coverage must be no more than 35% of the canvas
- protected highland and field swatches must be byte-identical
- protected swatch maximum CIE76 delta-E must be no more than 0.25
- edited forest/city/port swatch maximum CIE76 delta-E from H5 must be no more
  than 8.0, preserving the H5 material family while changing symbol grammar
- global RGB RMS difference from H5 must be no more than 24.0
- output luma entropy must remain between 96% and 106% of H5
- the edited forest luma entropy must be at least 6.0 bits
- city must contain at least 150 distinct flat building footprints, six
  courtyards, three street rings, and six radial/through streets
- port must contain at least 24 flat footprints and four flat pier centerlines
- canopy construction must contain at least 700 non-cloned irregular lobes,
  20 clearings, and 3,000 stipple or broken-line micro-marks
- boundary proxy and exact-repetition proxy must pass unchanged H4 definitions

Vision-only acceptance remains separate
The automated result is only a diagnostic handoff.  A reviewer must inspect
the native raster, 25% and 50% reductions, and 200% and 400% city, port,
forest, coast, field, highland, and boundary crops.  Any visible perspective,
directional shading, repeated round-canopy wallpaper, network break, or patch
seam rejects the candidate regardless of automated score.
